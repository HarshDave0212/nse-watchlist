NSE Daily Watchlist

Sector strength (% of stocks passing trend, liquidity and strength filters)
How to read this page
  • Score (0-100) blends trend, momentum, relative strength vs Nifty, RSI, MACD, volume, closeness to 52-week high and volatility, minus penalties for chasing stocks that already spiked. 75+ is BUY, 60-75 WATCH.
  • Price chart: price above the rising 50 (blue) and 200 (red) day lines means an uptrend. Price far above the 20-day line means stretched.
  • Volume bars: rising price on rising volume is more reliable than on thin volume.
  • Results: Strong = profit up over 10% and revenue up over 5% year on year. Weak = profit falling over 10% or a loss. Check results dates: a result inside 3 days makes the next day a gamble.
  • Entry/Stop/Target: stop is 1.5 ATR below, target 2 ATR above. Risk at most 1% of capital per trade. No signal guarantees next-day profit.

Educational screening output, not investment advice. Data: Yahoo Finance (free, may be delayed or incomplete).
